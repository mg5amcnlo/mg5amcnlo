3. Lineshape, per cell and per resonance.
   "after vs before"   the direct question: did the written sample
                       move when the bound moved?
   "vs weighted"       against decay_output = weighted, which makes no
                       accept/reject at all and is therefore the target
                       density with no bound in it.  Quoted for BOTH
                       campaigns, so the two can be read against each
                       other and against the reference itself.
                       PA        -> decay_output = weighted, no accept/reject
                       PAnojac   -> PA no-jac / joint -- NOT a weighted reference, see WEIGHTED in this file
                       madspin   -> decay_output = weighted, no accept/reject
   chi2 is on the 70 plot bins, dof = nbins - 1 (both densities are
   unit-area, one shared linear constraint).  KS is two-sample on the
   360-bin fine grid with effective sample sizes (sum w)^2/sum w^2.
   Both statistics ERR TOWARDS AGREEMENT here and neither is evidence
   on its own: every cell is decayed off the SAME production events, so
   two cells are positively correlated and an independent-errors chi2
   is conservative (several baseline rows sit below their dof for that
   reason), while the KS D is taken on a 360-bin grid and between
   correlated samples, both of which push its p-value up.  That is why
   the reference is a MEASURED floor and not a nominal 1.0.
   Read every chi2/dof against the baseline campaign's measured noise
   floor -- two replicas of ONE scheme at a second MadSpin seed gave
   139.2/138 and 135.7/138 combined over the two resonances, i.e.
   1.009 and 0.983 per dof.  Nothing at or below that is a signal.

   Breit-Wigner windows and bounds the RAMBO reshuffling jacobian,

   threshold) slice is where the bound and the weight it bounds are
   furthest apart, and where a bound that failed to dominate would
   show first.  Slices exist in the after campaign only: the

   decay_output = weighted, which is the stronger reference anyway.

5. Overweight counters (the PR-#375 safety net), per cell.
   A bound that dominates its weight can never be exceeded, so a
   per-event bound must read 0.  A NON-zero counter together with a
   lineshape shift is the signature of a bound that does not dominate;
   a non-zero counter alone means the excess was carried on the event
   weight instead of being clipped, which is exact but is still a
   weighted sample.
