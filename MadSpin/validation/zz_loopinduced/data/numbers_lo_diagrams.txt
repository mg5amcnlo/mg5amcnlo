one offline selection on all four: |m(l+l-) - 91.1880| < 36.6211 GeV and
pt(l+l-) > 1.0 GeV on both reconstructed pairs.  NEITHER cut is
applied at the generator level on any of them, so neither can see a z
on one side and a lepton pair on the other -- which is the asymmetry the
NLO comparison carries.  The one generator-level threshold there is, a
40 GeV floor on the same-flavour pairs, is measured inert below.
