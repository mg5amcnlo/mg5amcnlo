--- per-observable agreement over the bins where truth has support ---
  "rate" is the ratio of INTEGRALS over those bins, sum(mode)/sum(truth).
  It is deliberately not an inverse-variance weighted mean of the
  per-bin ratios: the per-bin error is built from the numerator, so a bin
  that fluctuates low gets a smaller error and more weight, and the mean
  comes out biased low -- about 5 % on a 2000-event pilot here, which is
  the size of the effects under study.  The integral ratio has no such
  bias.  "chi2/ndf" is the SHAPE test: the per-bin ratios against the
  best-fit flat line (i.e. against "rate"), so a pure normalisation
  offset does not enter it and only a genuine shape difference does.
