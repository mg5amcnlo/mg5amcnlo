-- unweighted vs weight-weighted <m_top>: the overweight cross-check --
   The mean above counts every written event ONCE, whatever weight it
   carries.  That is correct for a unit-weight sample, and the
   accept/reject schemes are meant to produce one -- but the overweight
   safety net writes some events with weight > 1 instead of rejecting
   them.  Where it does, the unweighted mean is not the physical mean,
   and a joint-vs-global_retry difference driven by THAT is a rate
   artefact of the accept/reject machinery, not a Z_k effect.

   Both columns are computed on the same binned support, so their
   difference isolates the weights and nothing else.  A cell with no
   overweights must show 0 to the last digit; a non-zero entry is the
   size of the contamination in this observable.
