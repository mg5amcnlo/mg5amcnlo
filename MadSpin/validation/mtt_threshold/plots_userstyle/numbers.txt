The truth and the MadSpin samples do NOT share a total cross section,
and the gap is expected.  MG5 truncates each top Breit-Wigner at
|m - m_t| < 15 Gamma_t (myamp.f, same convention as MadSpin BW_cut),
which removes 2.12% per resonance and 4.20% for the pair; MadSpin
normalises to sigma_production * BR and takes no such loss, and no
off-shell correction to the rate either.  Measured here:
