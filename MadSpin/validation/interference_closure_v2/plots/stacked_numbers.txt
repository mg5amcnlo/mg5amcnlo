Stacked renderings of the interference-closure figures.

Sign convention (also written on every stacked figure):
  in each bin, contributions with POSITIVE content are stacked
  upward from zero and contributions with NEGATIVE content are
  stacked downward from zero.  A band below the axis is a
  subtraction.  The top of the stack is therefore the sum
  of the POSITIVE contributions only and is NOT the total;
  the total is the algebraic sum of the two piles, drawn as
  an explicit line ("net: ...").  Read the line, not the
  envelope.

This matters because four of the nine blocks are positive in
every bin while the five interference ones are not: (I,I) is
negative over roughly half the range of C_nn and of dphi_lab,
and its sign change is the physics the figure exists to show.

Closure of the stack: the net of the stacked bands must equal,
bin by bin, the total curve drawn on the same panel.  It is the
same addition regrouped: the residual is floating point only.
