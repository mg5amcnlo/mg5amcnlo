ckk, cnn and crr are the PER-EVENT products
c_ij = cos(theta^i_l+) cos(theta^j_l-); every figure here is
a histogram of that product.  The spin-correlation
coefficient C_ij is its MEAN, one number per sample, which is
why the axis labels read "mean -> C_ij" and not "C_ij".  A
block with C_ij = 0 still has a cross section and a non-zero
histogram; what vanishes for it is the first moment.
