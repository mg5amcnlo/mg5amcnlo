Stacked renderings, user style.  Same data, same binning
and the same arithmetic as the MG7-style stacked figures
in ../plots: both call signed_stack() in
plot_interference.py, so the two renderings cannot
disagree.

SIGN CONVENTION (also written on every stacked figure):
  in each bin, contributions with POSITIVE content are stacked
  upward from zero and contributions with NEGATIVE content are
  stacked downward from zero, so a band below the axis is a
  subtraction and reads as one.  The top of the stack is
  therefore the sum of the POSITIVE contributions only and is
  NOT the total.  The total is the algebraic sum of the two
  piles and is drawn as an explicit line, labelled "net: ...".
  Read the line, not the envelope.

It is needed because the four diagonal blocks are positive in
every bin of every observable here while the five interference
blocks are not: (I,I) is negative over about half the range of
C_nn and of dphi_lab; that sign change is the whole effect.
