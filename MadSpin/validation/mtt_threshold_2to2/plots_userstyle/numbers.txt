THE POINT OF THIS STUDY.  For p p > t t~ j the off-shell spinmodes
populate m_tt < 2 m_t, because reshuffle_production rescales the recoil
jet and so moves the t t~ four-momentum.  With no jet there is nothing
to rescale: m_tt = sqrt(shat), which is what the reshuffle holds fixed.
The prediction is that EVERY spinmode is structurally empty below
2 m_t.  The two sections marked THE MEASUREMENT and BELOW 2 m_t are
where that is tested.

   No mode moves m_tt.  That is the result: for this production
   multiplicity the reshuffle holds the observable fixed, so all
   four modes inherit the production sample's m_tt exactly and all
   four carry the open-circle structural-zero mark below 2 m_t.

   Mechanism.  reshuffle_production hands every top-level final-state
   momentum to RAMBO mass_shuffle at fixed sqrt(shat).  Here the tops
   ARE the final state, so m_tt = sqrt(shat) identically and the
   reshuffle is holding the observable fixed by construction, whatever
   virtualities were drawn.  onshell never reshuffles at all.
   madspin_v1 rebuilds the point from the decay-chain topology holding
   shat and the production tree's invariants fixed -- and with two
   final-state particles the t t~ invariant IS shat.  Four different
   code paths, one kinematic reason.

-- what is left to separate the curves: the overweight events --------
   Every mode reproduces the production sample's event COUNTS bin for
   bin (above), so the only thing that can move a drawn density is the
   event WEIGHTS.  An unweighted MadSpin sample has unit weights except
   where the overweight safety net carried a trial weight that exceeded
   its accept/reject bound.  In units of one nominal event (553.2790 pb):

   mode        excess [events] of which < 2m_t+1 rel. sigma
   madspin              21.11          21.11    0.0021%
   PA                   69.53          62.13    0.0070%
   onshell               0.00           0.00    0.0000%
   madspin_v1            0.00           0.00    0.0000%

   That is the whole of the spread visible in the first bin above
   threshold, and it is an accept/reject artefact rather than a
   spinmode difference: onshell and madspin_v1 took no overweights at
   all and are therefore identical to the production sample and to
   each other, bin for bin AND weight for weight.  The excess is
   concentrated within ~1 GeV of 2 m_t because that is where the decay
   weight is hardest to bound: the pair has almost no phase space left
   for two off-shell tops.  It is carried, not clipped -- see each
   run's log in data/logs/ -- so it is in both the central values and
   the errors here.
