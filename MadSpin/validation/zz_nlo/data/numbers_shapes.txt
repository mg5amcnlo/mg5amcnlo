==============================================================================
THE DISCRIMINATION, RANKED
==============================================================================

chi2/ndf of a ratio against its own best-fit FLAT line: a pure
normalisation offset does not enter, so only a shape difference does.

  LI/NLO   is the number the question asks for.  (LI/LO)/(NLO/LO) is
           algebraically LI/NLO: the shared LO denominator cancels, which
           removes a fully correlated term and leaves two INDEPENDENT
           samples in the ratio.  chi2 = 1 means the two ratio shapes are
           the same to within the statistics of 50 000 events each.
  LI/LO    the box against the q q~ TREE, both 2 -> 2, so the extra parton
           of the NLO sample cannot contaminate it.
  NLO/LO   the K factor's own shape, for reference.
  factor   the spread of the normalised LI/NLO across the bins carrying
           more than 0.2 % of the rate: max/min, so 1.0 = no difference.

THE TOP THRESHOLD AT 2 m_t = 346.0 GeV
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The g g box runs through a top loop and must have a threshold there; the
q q~ tree has no analogue, so a step in LI/LO at 2 m_t would be a
box-specific feature and the strongest a-priori candidate of this study.
The fine m(ZZ) binning exists to look for it.  Fit over 250-450 GeV:

   LI/LO = a + b (m - 2 m_t) + c theta(m - 2 m_t)

LI/LO:
   level a         = 0.17048
   slope b         = -0.000234 per GeV
   STEP  c         = -0.01277 +- 0.00742   =  -7.5 % +- 4.4 % of the level
   significance    = 1.72 sigma
   chi2/ndf with the step 0.61, without it 0.68
LI/NLO:
   level a         = 0.11186
   slope b         = -0.000278 per GeV
   STEP  c         = +0.00070 +- 0.00537   =  +0.6 % +- 4.8 % of the level
   significance    = 0.13 sigma
   chi2/ndf with the step 1.43, without it 1.38

The Higgs triangle IS in the loop-induced sample: MadLoop builds an
off-shell h from the two z (VVS1_3 with MDL_MH / MDL_WH) and closes it
with a top or bottom Yukawa loop -- 4 of the 28 loop diagrams are
triangles, the other 24 boxes.  It cannot produce a peak here: the sample
has two ON-SHELL z, so m(ZZ) >= 2 m_Z = 182.4 GeV > m_h = 125 GeV and the
Higgs propagator is off shell at every point of the spectrum.  What is in
the sample is the smooth, non-resonant box-triangle interference, and
these samples cannot separate it from the box.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE OBSERVABLES THAT ARE TRAPS, AND WHY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

pt(ZZ) and Delta phi(Z,Z) are EXACTLY 0 and EXACTLY pi on both 2 -> 2
samples (measured pt(ZZ)_max = 0.0 on LO and 0.0 on LI, against 920.0 on
NLO).  They separate NLO from LO and LI instantly, for a reason that has
nothing to do with the box, and a ratio is undefined there.  Not plotted.

pt(Z_lead)/m(ZZ) is the borderline case: it is (beta/2) sin theta* on a
2 -> 2 sample and therefore bounded by 0.5 (measured maxima 0.4923 on LO,
0.4950 on LI), while NLO reaches 2.52.  3.66 % of the NLO weight sits above
the boundary and is off the axis.  Its topmost bins carry that artefact.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE COLLINS-SOPER FRAME BUYS NOTHING ON A 2 -> 2 SAMPLE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Event-by-event |cos theta*_CS| against the parent study's
|cos theta*| (harder Z in the ZZ rest frame, against the ZZ lab
direction):

sample       max |difference|      mean |difference|     pt(ZZ) max
lo                  2.220e-16              3.506e-17           0.00
li                  2.220e-16              3.499e-17           0.00
nlo                 9.950e-01              3.489e-02         920.05

They are the SAME observable on LO and LI, to double precision: a 2 -> 2
ZZ system has no transverse momentum, so its lab direction is the beam
axis and the Collins-Soper bisector degenerates onto it.  The CS frame
changes only the NLO curve, where the recoil tilts the ZZ direction.

Note also that the massless Collins-Soper shortcut
2(l1+ l2- - l1- l2+)/(Q sqrt(Q^2+qT^2)) is WRONG for two massive Z: it
returns beta cos theta*, which mixes the m(ZZ) dependence into what is
meant to be a pure angle and inflates the apparent discrimination by an
order of magnitude.  This module builds the frame explicitly instead.
