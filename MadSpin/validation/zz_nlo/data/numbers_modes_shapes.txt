reference: the directly generated off-shell four-lepton sample.

Every Z-level observable is reconstructed from the two lepton pairs and
is EXACT: the two Z are flavour tagged (one e+e-, one mu+mu-), so there
is no combinatorial ambiguity, and each observable is a function of the
pair four-momenta only.  The exception is the MASS: a reconstructed pair
is off shell where a produced z was not, so m_4l is not the production
m(ZZ) event by event -- which is the thing spinmode controls, not a
defect of the reconstruction.
