MadSpin polarisation weights on a showered NLO  p p > z z
==========================================================================
Three samples: the same process, the same run card and the same 250k
showered events, differing only in MadSpin's spinmode.  The
polarisation decomposition below is the REFERENCE (madspin, run_06)
sample's; the other two enter the distribution pane of each figure and
the cross-mode comparison section, and nowhere else.

--- IS THE DECAY CARD EXCLUSIVE IN EFFECT? (measured, not assumed) ---
The MadSpin card of all three runs is
  decay z > e+ e-
  decay z > mu+ mu-
which is exclusive in INTENT.  Whether it is exclusive in EFFECT is a
different question and it decides what these figures mean, so it is
read off the event record -- mark each z's end vertex, follow the chain
of status-44 copies, take the particles born at the final vertex -- for
every event of every file:

   spinmode      events one z->e+e- AND   at least one      other
                        other z->mu+mu-        z->e+e-   channels
   madspin       250000   250000   100%   250000   100%          0
   onshell       250000   250000   100%   250000   100%          0
   PA            250000   250000   100%   250000   100%          0

It is exclusive in effect: every event of every file has one z to e+e-
and the other to mu+mu-, with no other channel present at all.  So
BOTH observables have the full 250 000 events behind them before the
fiducial cuts, and the sparsity that made M(e+ mu+) undrawable on the
earlier decay z > light light samples (312 of 250 000 survivors) is
gone.

WHAT THE CROSS SECTIONS ARE OF.  Because the card is exclusive, the
sigma quoted for each file is
     sigma(p p > z z) x BR(z -> e+e-) x BR(z -> mu+mu-) x 2
(the 2 being the two ways of assigning the channels to the two z), and
NOT an inclusive p p > z z cross section.  The earlier inclusive
samples quoted ~13.1 pb, essentially the production cross section
because decay z > light light has a branching fraction of ~1; these
quote ~0.031 pb, smaller by ~2.35e-03, which is that product of
branching fractions.  The two sets of numbers are NOT comparable as
rates and only the ratios between the three modes are.

The two chi2 answer different questions and only the pair of them
is the result: a mode that differs from the reference ONLY in
normalisation gives a large "as drawn" chi2 and a small "shape
only" one, and reading the first as a shape difference would be
wrong.  Read both rows below before concluding anything.

how much is acceptance.  With the exclusive card f(z->ee) is 1 in

The sum over ALL events is 1 to within 0.017 % and 0.36 sigma.  That is
not a null result: the interference between different polarisations of
the same z is odd in the decay angles and integrates to zero over the
full angular phase space, so an unrestricted sum MUST come back to 1 and
the fact that it does is a check on the weights themselves.  The
interference is visible only differentially, or after a cut that
restricts the decay angles -- which every lepton selection does, and
which is why both selected rows above sit below 1 by many sigma where
the unrestricted one does not.
