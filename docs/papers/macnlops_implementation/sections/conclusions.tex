\section{Conclusions and the next accuracy conditions}
\label{sec:conclusions}
The current implementation provides a concrete multi-step construction:
qualified source generation, canonical first-step proposals, bounded
whole-event H removal/S veto, randomized accepted LHE collection and a
scale-preserving continuation. The source G completion, its bogus
low-scale factor, physical shower Sudakovs and the Delta source factor
are treated as different objects. Hard and MC mass conventions are
recorded separately. Complete source-sign reconstruction is based on
explicit native-history and Born-colour sums with certified flavour
grouping; an outer FKS integration Monte Carlo is not confused with those
sums.

The bounded rule has an exact algebraic interpretation: transfer
$C=\min(H_-,K_g)$, retain the hard residual $H_--C$, and reject eligible
S events with probability $C/K_g$. It avoids an invalid probability above
one without pretending that the unavailable compensation disappeared.
The resulting first-step difference is governed by the actual signed S
emission measure, not by the leading kernel alone. Negative S weights,
protected regions and distinct conditional source laws remain material.

The completed ten-million-source-event campaign demonstrates that all
five processes and both source settings can be processed through this
declared workflow and through the subsequent parton-shower validation.
The counting, normalization, covariance and stated kinematic checks pass.
It also shows sizeable residual differences for some processes and a
non-monotonic effect of Delta on the surviving negative H population.
These findings delimit the current method's numerical precision rather
than establishing unconditional positivity or exact shower equivalence.

A stronger equality construction would need the actual positive or
signed S emission density in the relevant conditional state, a matching
source negative measure, sufficient common support, and a common
continuation law. A stronger perturbative claim additionally needs the
leading-kernel/native-law correspondence, including maps, colour and
PDF/trial policies, to be controlled. Neither follows from adding a
single Sudakov factor to the existing ratio. The preserved common
sources, explicit H/S controls, portable profiles and full figure atlas
provide the information needed to distinguish those issues in subsequent
work, without hiding them in a survivor normalization.
