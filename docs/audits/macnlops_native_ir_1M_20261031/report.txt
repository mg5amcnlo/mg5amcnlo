Native-history MC@NLO-Delta with global infrared damping: 1M-event validation

Fresh generation: 1,000,000 events each for DY, gluon-fusion Higgs in HEFT and stable ttbar.
Source: delta_native_ir / mcatnlo-delta-native-ir-v1. Pythia source/libraries unchanged.
13 TeV pp; NNPDF40_nlo_as_01180/0 (331700); DY mee>60 GeV, mH125, mt173.
Seeds DY3220101, H3220201, ttbar3220301. DeltaCommonScale=true, DeltaBackwardISR=false.
First emission: stock Pythia8.318, alpha_s(MZ)=0.1365, one loop/noCMW, cutoffs0.5GeV.
Full shower: alpha_s(MZ)=0.118, one loop; MPI, hadronization, primordial kT, all QED,
and top/Higgs decays off. The setup matches the auxiliary-winner baseline.

Construction:
H keeps the native matching component c and starts at its get_qMC scale t_c, including
global-recoil FSR even when a local inverse map is invalid. No second history is drawn.
The common pre-unweighting source factor is the lower envelope of all-radiator Sudakovs
at native t_c and every valid singular reconstruction scale in its fixed Born context.
It reuses the source tables and PDF corrections without an emission-rate/PDF support gate.
A valid zero-Sudakov history gives exactly P=0 continuously, eliminating H and retaining
the paired real/MC contribution in S; H, S and G use the same P. Invalid sentinels do not
supply IR zeros. Unsupported hard remainders retain P=1; hard events without a passing
Born counterevent use the existing common maximum cap. Legacy winner samples cannot be relabelled.

mappings (3 processes x 3 campaigns x 50 jobs) pass all four inclusive-weight checks
within each printed HwU token's half-last-digit rounding precision. Higgs shower drift
decreases by 0.12508+/-0.03416 versus the winner sample (3.66sigma conditional MC error).
Native/winner DY and ttbar drift changes are not statistically significant.
Before-shower uncertainties are finite-event sampling estimates; first realized also
includes rejection draws. Full-shower errors use correlated differences over 50 split jobs.
Channel integration and beta-normalization errors are separate. Samples are independent;
both source damping and continuation changed, so the campaign contrast alone is not a
controlled causal test. Sparse unsampled transfer numerators do not establish physical
zero rates with zero error; their Gaussian ratio inference is suppressed in migration_summary.
The archived original-v4 ttbar shower had QEDByOther on; winner/native have all QED off.
Thus original-v4 ttbar comparisons also include that setting correction.

Interpretation:
This removes the auxiliary continuation-history mismatch and substantially improves the
Higgs shower migration. It does not establish exact cancellation. ttbar still has a
0.555% inclusive shift and full H/S=0.93895+/-0.00763 at 20-50GeV and 0.93344+/-0.00935
at 50-100GeV; expected first-emission ratios there are 0.93773+/-0.00585 and 0.91696+/-0.00762.
These are substantial pre-existing transfer deficits, rather than statistically resolved
new shower drifts. DY retains a statistically resolved expected inclusive transfer too.
This validation does not determine the remaining residuals' cause. Assess the measured
tables/plots. The source damping is a
fixed-native-Born prescription; the source tables differ from the exact competing shower,
and channel-average beta plus integration errors remain approximations. The IR endpoint
at 0.5 GeV is a source-table continuation, not a finite-cutoff shower probability identity.
