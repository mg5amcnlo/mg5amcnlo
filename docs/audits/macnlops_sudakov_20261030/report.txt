MAcNLOPS source/shower Sudakov correction — 30 October 2026

Implemented policy: component-sudakov-ratio-v4 (default).
Legacy --s-veto v3 and v2 remain available.
Pythia and the production source Delta are unchanged.

The source keeps its per-connection reconstructed stopping scales and shipped
Sudakov tables. Its Delta is averaged over the unconditional native starting-
scale distribution. The shower denominator uses a common winning evolution
scale on every Born radiator and the conditional start distribution Q > t;
K already contains the probability of Q > t.

A = E[Delta_src(real;Q)], P = E[Delta_PS(Born;t,Q) | Q>t].
u=max(D_G-R,0), C=min(u,K), F=beta*K_sh*P, T=min(C*A,F).
S rejection=T/F, H removal=T/(u*A), with explicit zero-source handling.
The common bound reduces H removal whenever the S rejection saturates.

ISR uses the resolved-flavour backward PDF convolution, finite-z support,
actual shower PDF freezing and shower alpha_s. It includes the difference
between NLO PDF evolution and the LO shower, beyond an endpoint PDF ratio.
Final quarks use the massive global-recoil Pqq integral, including the
post-branch mass-reduced-pT cutoff. This is required for top quarks.
The remaining final-gluon denominator uses the existing tables; none of
these three processes has a final Born gluon.

Independent quark-shower reference:
https://gitlab.com/Pythia8/releases/-/raw/pythia8318/src/SimpleTimeShower.cc
Only a read-only reference was downloaded; installed Pythia was not edited.

Physics scope and remaining discrepancy:
The large small-pT Sudakov imbalance is removed. This does not establish
exact local transfer closure: beta=sigma_S/sigma_B is still a channel
average; local S/B variations and Born hard-scale coupling/luminosity
conversions remain approximations, as in v3. The independent Sudakov
checks do not validate those other factors.

The ttbar expected net is -5.0436 pb; the realized net is -4.4930 pb.
The expected difference is statistically resolved, so it must not be
described as a fluctuation or complete normalization closure.
Its ISR and FSR contributions are -4.7915 and -0.2521 pb, respectively.
It is concentrated at larger first-emission pT; shower migration also
leaves a residual low-pT shape difference (see range table and plots).
The correction is therefore validated as a Sudakov correction, with
the remaining transfer-normalization approximation stated explicitly.
