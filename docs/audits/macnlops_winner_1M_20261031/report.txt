Validation exposed and fixed two matching bugs:
1. A negative source H hard remainder with zero resolved shower-PDF density
   has F=0 and no common transfer; H must be retained. Zero normalization
   previously aborted. Negative/nonfinite normalization remains an error.
2. Source auxiliary-history kernels must use the representative flavour IDs
   used by grouped source generation. Resolved charm/strange/bottom IDs
   previously produced false hard-remainder source averages. The actual
   shower denominator continues to use resolved physical flavours.
Both have regression tests. All final channels were rematched with both fixes.
A third scale-only edge case affected five positive ttbar H hard remainders
without a Born counterevent. Their common hard fallback now uses the largest
pre-existing hard scale. Weights/momenta were preserved exactly, and ttbar
was rematched and reshowered after that deterministic cap repair. The source
weight functions were unchanged. Invalid intermediate results are archived
under validation/pre_grouped_profile_fix and excluded from final numbers.

Interpretation and limits:
The implementation now passes generation, probability, provenance, unweighting
and stock-shower transport checks at the requested statistics. This does not
establish exact cancellation. Inspect the transfer tables and PDF plots:
common H caps can still have a different conditional distribution from the
actual S winning scales. In Higgs pT5-20GeV, transfer-weighted mean starts
S=28.282GeV and H=17.699GeV (H matching-component scale=28.066GeV). This
points to a conditioning difference: the H posterior approximates the overall
first-emission history density, while vetoed S histories are transfer weighted.
The means do not establish the sole cause of the observed shower migration.
The history prior is fixed-native-Born AP(z)/t^2 with coupling/PDF corrections;
alternative inverse Born states, their matrix elements/Jacobians and exact
massive/global-recoil proposal kernels are approximated. The S/B normalization
is still a channel average beta. Statistically resolved residuals must not
be described as fluctuations or exact matching closure.

The previous ttbar full-shower comparison used QEDByOther=on unintentionally;
the new sample disables it. Comparisons with that archived ttbar shower
combine this configuration correction with the source/scale change.
