Historical meanings of SpaceShower:pdfMode and the archived tables
27 October 2026

Historical source snapshot: df4187461e8d10f05e78987dd8de5f02fe4e719a,
the commit adding sudakov_r515_PDFmode[012].f.gz on 8 August 2019.
All historical source paths below are relative to that commit. The Pythia
source prefix is Pythia/Pythia/pythia-src/ (locally modified Pythia8.225).

The numbers label evolution prescriptions, not three choices of PDF set.

PDFmode 0: ordinary backward-evolution no-emission probability.
  The ISR branching kernels retain mother/daughter PDF ratios and Bjorken-x
  restrictions. See src/SpaceShower.cc:919,1060-1062,1096-1097,1169-1170,
  1392,1461-1468. It consequently depends on the PDF set and Bjorken x.
  SudGen/pythia_interface_for_sudgen.cc:92-93 supplies x=0.1 to the standard
  table-generation call (the separate _x wrapper accepts an explicit x).
  This is not a generic PDF-free Sudakov to which the current caller can
  simply attach its own event-dependent PDF ratio.

PDFmode 1: PDF-free forward Sudakov, momentum-sum-rule prescription.
  The source changes the non-diagonal kernels from backward to forward
  evolution and replaces PDF values/ratios by1. It sets zMinAbs=1e-5,
  xMaxAbs=1 and isValence=false, then multiplies the accepted splitting
  weight by z. See src/SpaceShower.cc:926,928,1080-1084,1151-1166,
  1287-1294,1352-1365,1379,1461-1468.
  Schematically its z integral is integral dz z sum_b P_ba(z), with the
  stated numerical lower cutoff and the shower's actual kinematic limits.

PDFmode 2: PDF-free forward Sudakov, restricted-z prescription.
  It uses the same forward-kernel/PDF-removal changes, but sets
  zMinAbs=0.5 (src/SpaceShower.cc:927) and applies no extra factor z.
  Schematically its z integral is integral_(1/2)^(zmax) dz sum_b P_ba(z).

These meanings are documented explicitly in
share/Pythia8/xmldoc/SpacelikeShowers.xml:421-424. The historical wrapper
selects SpaceShower:pdfMode=2 at SudGen/pythia_interface_for_sudgen.cc:76.
The wrapper changes only SpaceShower:pdfMode; TimeShower:pdfMode remains
at its default0. The physical FI initial-recoiler PDF-weight block exists,
but the dedicated dummy API recoiler has mother1=0 and its prepared ends
have isrType=0; that block is therefore bypassed for these table trials.
This was verified subsequently from both historical/current source and
actual prepared ends (see dipole_normalization/source_audit.json and
all_dipole_normalization/fsr_colour_line_trials.json in the audit).
All FSR grids in the three archives are exactly identical.

Mapping to the published prescription:
The paper's AppendixA has three strategies, whose numbering is NOT the
PDFmode numbering. Strategy1 is the symmetric z interval with an explicit
one-half factor. Strategy2, eqs.A.14-A.15, is the restricted-z integral,
matching PDFmode2. Strategy3, eqs.A.16-A.17, is the z-weighted integral,
matching PDFmode1. The paper explicitly selects strategy3 for its
pre-tabulation because it agreed most closely with backward showering.
Thus PDFmode1 matches the documented paper choice, although this archived
wrapper selects PDFmode2. The paper treats their differences as O(epsilon).
Sources: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2002.12716#page=42 (printed page41)
         https://arxiv.org/pdf/2002.12716#page=43 (printed page42).

Two separate factors must not be confused: Na=2 in eq.3.19 compensates
the symmetric sum over daughter labels. The separate N_l(a) in eq.3.17
counts the emitter's colour lines:1 for a quark,2 for a gluon. Strategies2
and3 remove the need for the Na symmetry factor; they do not remove the
gluon colour-line factor. See also the explicit one-line statement in
AppendixA, printed page40, and eqs.3.17-3.19, printed pages12-13.

Independent normalization evidence in the historical implementation:
ISR prepare creates one SpaceDipoleEnd per incoming radiator
(src/SpaceShower.cc:380-385). The trial pTnext uses only beamA, once
(842-844). However its gluon kernel retains full-radiator strength:
g2gInt has coefficient6 (1070-1071), and the acceptance weight is
[1-z(1-z)]^2 (1246-1248). The resulting kernel is
6[1-z(1-z)]^2/[z(1-z)], with soft coefficient2CA=6. The quark soft
coefficient is2CF=8/3 (1122-1123,1313). No extra factor1/2 for a gluon's
colour lines appears. Restricting z>0.5 does not halve that coefficient.

For FSR, the dummy event contains two final radiators in FF and one in FI
(src/TimeShower.cc:1917,1949-1979). Preparation includes all final entries
(644-649), and trial evolution loops over all dipole ends (2122-2171).
The local colour coefficients are CF for quarks and CA/2 for each gluon
colour end (2379). Thus the returned FF probability covers the entire
two-radiator system; the returned FI probability covers one full radiator.
src/Merging.cc:416-438 returns these probabilities directly, and
SudGen/gridsudgen.f:605-607 stores them without roots or other conversion.
The present caller instead multiplies table factors over the emitter's
colour connections and applies one external ISR PDF ratio:
Template/NLO/SubProcesses/montecarlocounter.f:1514-1597.

Scope of the conclusion:
PDFmode1 is the prescription described for the paper's pre-tabulation;
PDFmode2 is the choice in the inspected historical generation wrapper.
Neither fact proves that an archived table has the per-colour-line
normalization required by the present caller. No committed generation log
identifies the exact executable that produced the archives. The historical
source and numerical FF/full-radiator comparisons indicate a separate
normalization issue that a PDFmode selection alone cannot resolve.
This audit inspected source and existing results; no shower was run.
