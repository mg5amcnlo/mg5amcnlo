MC@NLO-Delta versus Pythia first-QCD-emission no-emission probability
27 October 2026; branch MCcntRefactor_Sfun, commit 801a3417c98335f33894376df6e1005221ec53a6

Result
======
The shipped Delta construction is not a close numerical approximation to
Pythia's first-emission survival probability for this ttbar sample. In the
20--75 GeV first-emission range its mean is roughly 3--5 times smaller.
The gg channel drives the deficit. The qqbar channel behaves differently;
its excess can mask the gg deficit at low pT. Changing the shower PDF from
the branch's built-in fallback PDF13 to the matching hard-PDF family PDF15
does not explain the discrepancy. No production physics source was changed.

  10--20           0.05738        0.15055         0.381 +/- 0.037
  20--30           0.06241        0.28045         0.223 +/- 0.021
  30--50           0.09655        0.46215         0.209 +/- 0.012
  50--75           0.20581        0.65529         0.314 +/- 0.009
  75--100          0.36973        0.78463         0.471 +/- 0.009
 100--150          0.54285        0.86522         0.627 +/- 0.010
 150--200          0.64613        0.90391         0.715 +/- 0.019

The pT is the shower evolution pT after the first ACCEPTED QCD branching,
not the lab transverse momentum of its emitted parton or pT(ttbar).
Each row compares the means on the SAME emission-selected ensemble. For
Born state B (momenta, colour, starting scales), independently shower it
240 times to estimate F_B(q)=Pr(pT_first<=q | B). No-emission showers have
pT_first=0. Use 60 further independent showers to supply real configurations
R and q; evaluate Delta(B,R) with native branch stopping scales, then bin
Delta(B,R) and F_B(q) together. There are 52185 emitting probes; 75 of 52260
probe showers had no emission. All 300 repetitions per Born were accepted.
Ratios are ratios of the binned means, not means of individual ratios.
Uncertainties use 500 bootstrap resamples of the 871 Born clusters, keeping
all probes and references belonging to a Born together. Bins are correlated.
The CSV contains every bin and separate gg/qqbar results. Very sparse
bootstrap bins with zero reference denominators have an unavailable ratio
error (empty CSV field), rather than an invented finite uncertainty.

Direct checks on the unchanged shipped table
===========================================
Independent direct Fortran evaluation, without the Python evaluator, at
start 200 GeV and dipole mass 500 GeV gives incoming-gluon line ratios:
 q=10: 0.0005249125; q=20: 0.01068364; q=50: 0.17903986.
For two incoming gluons with two identical lines each, the corresponding
products before PDFs are 7.59e-14,1.30e-8,0.00102754. These tiny values are
present in the shipped table; the arguments are correctly in GeV.
At q=10,mDip=1000 the table returns literal zero. Source data contain zero
plateaux; this is not floating-point underflow from our analysis.

The top FI table has 4777 of 5000 nodes exactly 1. Its nontrivial support is
restricted to mDip=1.435--25.851 GeV and q=1--8.554 GeV. Consequently the
sampled ttbar-scale FI factors give unity. Table metadata m_top=0 alone is
not proof of massless top kernels: the generator skips top mass assignment,
while current Pythia's dummy FSR API builds massless trial particles. The
historical generation settings of the shipped table are not recorded.
We do not infer a unique historical cause, alpha_s, or PDF mode from the
current generator. No regenerated table was substituted in this test.

The production gluon weighted average also has an edge case when both
line ratios are 1: gl1=gl2=0 and the raw normalized combination is 0/0.
This occurred for 40/52185 real probes, and no common-cut points. The
compiled gfortran-O2 min/max returned 0 there; that value is compiler-
dependent, not a well-defined probability. The CSV flags these cases and
also reports the result excluding them. In the quoted20--75 GeV bins the
exclusion changes the ratio by less than 0.0001 and cannot explain the
large discrepancy. At 150--200 the ratio changes 0.715 -> 0.728. Finite
production probability clipping is retained and separately counted.

fixed muR=muF=173 GeV, native nn23nlo hard PDF. Generation seed 102701;
1000 total NLO-Delta events, accuracy request 0.03, four workers. Matrix-
element/MC tests and all subprocess pole checks pass. Integral 754.0 +/- 4.1 pb.
FKS_params.dat:
  MCExplicitKLSum = .false.          (sum-kl false)
  FKSExplicitSum = .false.           (sum-ij false)
  MCSubtractionAtFixedFlow = .true.  (single-colour-flow true)
The retained Born ensemble is 871 S events: 785 gg, 86 qqbar, 55 negative weights.
All |weights| are identical. The reported averages use the ABSOLUTE S-event
measure, equally weighted, to test conditional probabilities. They are not
signed NLO cross sections or probabilities extracted from signed weights.
Written sampled starting scales and directed dipole tags are preserved.
The pure ttbar Born reference scale is mT(top), damped over [0.1,1] mT.

Pythia8.313: actual shower with branch QCD settings: alpha_s(MZ)=.118,
one-loop running, CMW off; pTmaxMatch=1 and fudge=1; globalRecoil on,
globalRecoilMode=2, nMaxGlobalRecoil=1, nMaxGlobalBranch=1,limitPTmaxGlobal on;

PDF15 control uses NNPDF2.3 NLO alpha_s=.119. These PDF alpha_s labels

scale reconstruction are 0. QED, MPI, hadronization, primordial kT and top
decays are off, isolating production QCD radiation. MECs are off, as in
the matching prescription. With the default MEextended=false, top
production in this 2->2 process also has MEtype=0 when MECs are enabled.

Scope and interpretation
========================
The real-emission diagnostic evaluates the branch's Delta formula on
Pythia first-emission momenta, using that shower's Born preimage/colour
and the saved branch starting scales. It does not reevaluate the full
FKS integrand or all alternative native-history Born maps. Real shower
light-parton masses are retained; native reconstruction uses the hard
model's masses. This distinction can matter near the low-pT cutoff.
The common-cutoff test separately removes the unequal-real-stop ambiguity.
Both are probability validations on a stated ensemble, not a proof or
failure of NLO accuracy, nor a comparison of final jet distributions.
History-dependent Delta is not required to equal a one-dimensional CDF.
The results quantify how close it is in the requested first-emission test.

Reproduction
============
The exact source and cards are retained; no external services are needed.
Build a fresh NLO export of the process above with this branch (bin/mg5_aMC),
copy cards/ into its Cards directory (the four *_card.dat/FKS_params.dat
files), and generate the parton-only tt_delta run. The archived export.cmd
and launch.cmd show the original commands/paths; replace paths as needed.
To reproduce this SAME finite ensemble, decompress ttbar_S.lhe.gz and run:

bash tests/validation/delta_noemission/reproduce.sh \
  /export/tmp/rikkert/Pythia/pythia8313 \
  /export/tmp/rikkert/delta_noemission_20261027/TT \
  /export/tmp/rikkert/delta_noemission_20261027/ttbar_S.lhe \
  /path/to/new/output

The script compiles the probe and exact Delta/PDF libraries, showers the
fixed input with all four saved seeds, audits records, evaluates probabilities
and produces the plots/CSVs. It requires gfortran,C++17,NumPy,Matplotlib.
PDF/grid and source/library hashes are in the provenance files. Large raw
trial files can be replayed in analyse.py with --reuse to regenerate plots
from the saved analysis.npz without further showers.
