Interpretation:
The stronger gluon deficit is consistent with the independently identified
full-radiator versus per-colour-line issue, but these averages do not isolate
its cause. The reference uses alphaS=.118 while historicalgeneration settings
suggest larger couplings; PDFs, version and massive-top treatment also differ.
Increasing the shower couplings generally lowers fixed-cut no-emission
probabilities and hence increases a fixed Delta/reference ratio. Actual-real
bins change their sampled configurations too, so monotonic improvement of
every binned ratio is not guaranteed.

The Delta construction is an approximation to the shower probability, using
particular scales, colour flow and PDF treatment. Exact equality of all these
averages is not a theorem; changes should be assessed with matched settings
and topology-sensitive checks rather than choosing the table closest to1.
No assumption that the old default is valid enters this comparison.
No new shower, production-code change, or table transformation was made.
