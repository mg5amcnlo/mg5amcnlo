Mode 1 investigation: conclusions

The unchanged PDFmode1 archive is much closer than the default, but it does
not reproduce the present Simple shower to a few percent. With the known
historical couplings (ISR 0.130, FSR 0.1365, one-loop running, CMW off), the
ratio of mean Delta to mean conditional shower no-emission probability on
actual first-emission configurations is about 0.79 in the 20-50 GeV bins,
rising to 0.91 at 100-150 GeV. Changing the shower PDF from 13 to 15 does
not resolve the deficit. With both couplings at 0.118, the ratios are
0.70-0.74 at 20-50 GeV. Detailed values and errors are in report.txt.

The deficit is mainly in gg. At 30-50 GeV with the increased couplings and
PDF13, the ratio is 0.770 +/- 0.005 for gg, versus 0.972 +/- 0.016 for
qqbar. A control retaining the original probes and changing only the
reference shower confirms that the coupling increase helps, but does not
explain the full deficit.

The auxiliary common-cutoff test assigns the same stopping scale to every
live colour line. At 20, 50 and 100 GeV, unchanged mode 1 gives 0.308,
0.656 and 0.916 times the new PDF13 reference. These numbers describe a
different test from the actual first-emission bins.

An isolated diagnostic takes the square root of only the incoming-gluon
table factors, leaving quarks, FSR and the production caller unchanged.
For the common-cutoff test against the new PDF13 reference, the ratios
become 0.934, 1.043 and 1.016 at 20, 50 and 100 GeV. With PDF15 they are
0.923, 1.035 and 1.010. This strengthens the full-radiator versus
per-colour-line normalization hypothesis. It is not a validated general
replacement prescription. See gluon_normalization*.json/txt.

The new references comprise 522,600 successful Simple Pythia 8.313 showers
on the same 871 stable-ttbar Born states at 13 TeV. Runtime checks confirm
Simple shower classes. Every new Born record exactly matches the baseline;
common-cut Delta is bitwise unchanged. The original 300-trial replay and
serial/parallel Delta checks pass. Actual showers use ordinary backward
PDFmode0; mode 1 labels only the lookup table.

The couplings match historical source, not an exact archived-generation
log. Version, table-mass and other generation differences remain. No
production source or selected production table was changed.
