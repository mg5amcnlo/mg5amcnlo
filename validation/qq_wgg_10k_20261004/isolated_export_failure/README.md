# Stopped isolated-process export

The initial `u d~ > w+ g g [QCD]` export in `/tmp/mg5-qq-wgg-10k-80ef8o8z` was stopped during MINT grid adaptation after investigating whether its FKS partition was complete. It produced no finalized event sample. Its intermediate integration estimates and CPU usage are excluded from the MINT/AmpliCol comparison.

This directory retains the process and launch commands, settings, cards, initial source hashes, and interrupted worker logs. See [status.json](status.json) for the archived worker inventory. The isolated export omitted Born flavour configurations needed by the global FKS partition: real-emission sector 6 (`u d~ > W+ d d~ g`) had only the `(5,4)` pair, giving its partition weight one. A soft gluon could therefore remain unsuppressed while the two hard quark jets passed the jet cuts, without the missing `W+ d d~` Born counterpart to supply the required cancellation. The replacement benchmark retains the complete `p p > W+ j j [QCD]` export and its foreign Born support, then selects the requested `u d~ > W+ g g` Born component for generation. Its reported rate is a component of that complete partition, not an inclusive physical W+2-jet cross section.

The archived [FKS table](diagnostic_sources/fks_info.inc) gives real PDGs `(2, -1, 24, 1, -1, 21)` and `FKS_J_FROM_I_D(6,5)={4}` for this sector. [fks_Sij.f](diagnostic_sources/fks_Sij.f) (lines 183–204) constructs its one-term denominator; [fks_singular.f](diagnostic_sources/fks_singular.f) (line 624) evaluates the full real matrix element; [cuts.f](diagnostic_sources/cuts.f) (lines 435 and 562) counts all three real partons and accepts two jets. The generated tests of the included soft/collinear limits and IR poles passed; they do not test this omitted limit.

Verification of the replacement export found eight integrated sectors and 30 total sectors including the foreign Born aliases. Sector 6 now contains `(5,2)`, `(5,4)`, `(6,1)`, `(6,2)`, `(6,4)`, and `(6,5)`, restoring the omitted gluon limits. The selected subprocess groups `u d~` and `c s~` initial states in this beam ordering.
