Statistics and correctness review, 2026-10-08

Scope
Read-only inspection of the current native sampler and saved 300K ttbar runs
with nevt_job=2500 and 30000. No physics run or production source change.
Reproduce the saved worker/epoch diagnostics with statistics/analyze.py.

Rate algebra
simple_integrator.f90 native_consider records every attempted contribution,
including rejected and zero points, in per-epoch and total first/second
moments. native_rates reports sqrt(M2)/N. ampli_pool.combine_native_rates
combines the survey once plus every epoch from every worker, weighting by
trial counts and including between-epoch differences. The result is the
pooled-trial moment identity (apart from the already summarized survey).
There is no apparent lost probability factor or dropped-production-epoch
mistake in this path. Never estimate rates only from retained event candidates.

With a fixed number of draws, predictable proposal adaptation preserves the
importance-sampling mean when the exact sampling density is used and support
is preserved. This fact alone does not establish unbiasedness or calibrated
errors after data-dependent quota/tail stopping. The production comments
already state that nonzero-count stopping is not claimed unbiased. Blindly
inverse-variance-weighting noisy epoch estimates would introduce another
data-dependent weighting issue; it is not a safe drop-in improvement.

What the saved discrepancy does and does not show
Published signed rates differ by 2.329880 pb, 2.87799 nominal quadrature errors.
Absolute rates differ by 3.001592 pb, 3.12650 nominal errors. The gg contributions
account for the signed shift: GF3 +1.03034 pb, GF2 +1.21407 pb, GF1 +0.29845 pb;
the other two channels each move by about -0.106 pb. Their individual nominal
signed significances are 1.99, 2.30, 1.20, -0.72, and -0.69 respectively.

The shared survey induces approximately 0.004769 pb^2 signed covariance
through its direct rate contribution. Subtracting twice this from the naive
difference variance changes its standard deviation from 0.809552 to 0.803639
pb. This direct covariance is small, but reuse of generation seeds and related
adaptive histories produces additional covariance not determined here.
One pair of runs cannot establish bias or validate a quoted confidence level.

Nonzero stopping is an unlikely explanation specific to this ttbar sample:
only 55 out of 3,074,406 and 80 out of 3,064,052 attempted draws were zero.
Most epochs therefore effectively have predetermined trial counts once they
start. The number and sizes of epochs, and termination on the overweight
criterion, are nevertheless data dependent.

Within-channel independent-worker diagnostics do not show broad undercoverage
in the old run's dominant channels. For gg/GF3 (55 workers), worker-scatter
error is 0.38216 pb versus pooled-moment 0.36279 pb; nominal chi-square scatter
p=0.24370. For gg/GF2 (54 workers), 0.33552 versus 0.37007 pb; p=0.72378.
The small ux_u channel has p=0.01084 with seven workers, and the larger-job
gg/GF3 has p=0.04135 with five workers. These isolated diagnostics warrant
calibration, not a conclusion of bias, especially with several comparisons.
Cluster estimates from only one or five workers are themselves very noisy.
There is no common monotone early-to-late mean drift across the gg channels.

Selection and adaptation constraints
Native weights are f/q_birth; historical envelope reevaluation multiplies
by the ratio of proposal Jacobians at the saved physical coordinates.
Changing only unfolded coordinate maps preserves the current reweighting
contract. Changing discrete MC choices, real/virtual stream probabilities,
folding, or other physics sampling also requires recording and accounting
for those likelihood ratios. Their current frozen status cannot simply be
removed while keeping a coordinate-only history representation.

The storage cutoff does not itself impose an integrand-weight cap: every
weight above it is retained, and smaller weights have known random acceptance.
Final thresholds must remain at least that cutoff. Raising an envelope after
observing a tail requires reconsidering earlier selected events and preserving
the native correction factors, as done now. Clipping large weights, deleting
rare tail points, or certifying from accepted events alone would be wrong.

The <1% checks bound observed full tail mass, reserve mass, and worst collected
subset mass under their recorded thresholds. They do not constitute a 99%
statistical guarantee that unseen phase space contains less than 1% mass.
The source explicitly describes positive-tolerance rank selection as a finite
quota approximation. Improvements should keep this limitation explicit and
validate event distributions as well as inclusive cross sections. The saved
LHE correction factors barely reduce absolute effective event count:
299,518/300,000 for 2500/job and 299,396/300,000 for 30000/job. Hence optimizing
these tiny correction factors alone cannot materially improve sample power.

Recommended validation before adopting a large efficiency improvement
1. Compare changed and current methods over independently seeded analytic
   targets covering signed cancellations, sparse support, narrow/heavy tails,
   folded coordinates, and quota/top-up boundaries. Measure rate bias,
   empirical RMS versus reported error, 68/95% coverage, and weighted shape
   moments. Unit-test identities and a single high-statistics shape check do
   not calibrate adaptive stopping.
2. Run several independent physics seeds, preferably 10-20 moderate samples,
   with fresh surveys. Compare means and empirical dispersion, and rerun both
   job sizes or algorithms per seed. Paired common-random-number comparisons
   should use measured paired differences, not assume independent errors.
3. Include a fixed-trial diagnostic integration against the same known or
   high-statistics reference. This is a validation control, not a required
   third production stage. It separates estimator/stopping behavior from
   unweighting/collection effects.
4. Judge useful efficiency using final signed effective events per CPU and
   per wall time, as well as requested events per matrix-element evaluation;
   do not trade a purported speedup for changed shape, corrections, or hidden
   error undercoverage. Preserve every attempted point in the rate estimator.
